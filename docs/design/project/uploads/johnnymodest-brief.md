# JohnnyModest Consultancy — Branding & Website Brief

## Project overview

Rebuild of johnnymodest.com as a Next.js site. The goal is a clean, direct, distinctive consultancy presence for a senior product leader who goes by Johnny Modest. No fluff. No packages. No theater.

---

## Brand positioning

**Core USP:** Direct, no-nonsense senior product leadership. The client brings cross-domain pattern recognition and execution speed — not deep vertical expertise in one industry.

**Tagline (working):** Zero-nonsense consulting.

**Brand philosophy:** Built around the Zero Nonsense principle. Plain language, uncomfortable conversations sooner rather than later, data-driven decisions, no buzzwords as a substitute for clear thinking. The site should embody this — not just describe it.

**Tone:** Direct, warm without being soft, occasionally dry humor. No jargon. No "holistic approaches," "move the needle," "synergy," or "investment" in place of "cost."

**What we don't say out loud but the site should imply:** You hire Johnny because he'll see things your team can't see from the inside. He leverages your assets better than you currently are.

---

## Services (three, no more)

1. **Product Strategy & GTM** — strategy work, go-to-market planning, product direction
2. **Hands-on Product Leadership** — parachutes in, course corrects, builds systems that run without him, then exits. Not day-to-day management. Fix and leave.
3. **Tooling & Workflow Setup** — helps teams work faster by setting up the right tools and processes. Modern stack including AI tooling, without leading with the buzzword.

## Visual direction

### Overall aesthetic
Scandinavian minimal. Stark, clean, lots of whitespace. Oversized type. No stock photography. No hero images. Typography and one accent color do all the work.

### Color
- **Primary accent:** Amber 400 — `#EF9F27`
- **Text on amber:** Amber 900 — `#412402`
- **Dark amber (hover/borders):** `#854F0B`
- **Light amber (fills/backgrounds):** `#FAEEDA`
- Background: white / near-white. Black text. The amber is used generously — not as a shy highlight but as a genuine brand color. Retina-scraping is acceptable and intentional.
- **Color system logic:** The rest of the site is black and white. The amber-brown palette is self-contained: amber for fills and accents, dark brown for text on amber backgrounds. Never dark grey on amber — brown keeps it cohesive within the same color family.

### Typography
- The current logo uses a generic typeface — will be redesigned by the client
- Body and UI: a clean geometric sans (Inter, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, or Syne — client to finalize)
- Oversized type for headlines (42px+ on desktop)
- Weight: 500 for headings, 400 for body. Nothing heavier.
- Sentence case throughout. No all-caps except small labels/tags.

### Logo
- The J/M monogram mark is worth keeping — distinctive and clean
- The wordmark "JOHNNY MODEST" in caps is strong — keep it
- The box/border treatment around the current logo may be dropped or simplified for the new minimal direction
- Typeface will be updated by the client — direction: geometric, distinctive, not generic

### No-go list
- Stock photography
- Hero images
- Gradients, shadows, blur
- Rounded-corner excess
- Generic "consulting" iconography
- Emoji in UI

## Key interactive element: Before/After text slider

A drag-to-reveal slider — but instead of photos, it shows a paragraph of business language before and after removing the fluff.

**Example:**

- Before: *"Leveraging synergistic cross-functional alignment to holistically drive stakeholder value across the product ecosystem."*
- After: *"We got the teams talking to each other. The product shipped on time."*

The "After" panel uses the amber background (`#EF9F27`) with dark text (`#412402`). This is the signature element of the home page — it proves the brand promise visually.
